# Problem Analysis

## Problem Statement
The Government of Jharkhand (Department of Higher & Technical Education) seeks a "Multilingual Voice-First Societal Challenge & Innovation Collaboration Platform" under Theme: Smart Education (SIH26043). 
There is currently no structured channel for citizens to report local societal problems (infrastructure, water, agriculture) and have them transformed into actionable, university-led projects with industry backing and measured outcomes.

## Background & Pain Points
- Citizens across Jharkhand identify real local problems constantly but lack an accessible channel to report them.
- Existing forms with complex classification dropdowns are a barrier for rural citizens.
- English-only or typed-Hindi-only platforms exclude citizens speaking tribal languages (Santali, Mundari, Ho, Kurukh).
- Even if problems are reported, there is no structured pipeline to route them to capable university teams, faculty, and industry partners for resolution.
- It is difficult to track the lifecycle from "problem reported" to "solution piloted and verified."

## Target Users
- Citizens (community reporters)
- Government Administrators (Higher & Technical Education Dept.)
- University Administrators
- Faculty Mentors
- Students
- Industry Partners
- Platform Administrators

## Expected Solution
A workflow platform with AI embedded at every step (not a chatbot) that:
1. Allows citizens to speak their problem in their own language.
2. Uses AI to transcribe, translate, and structure the problem into a formal challenge.
3. Enables government validation and duplicate detection.
4. Matches the challenge to universities and faculty based on capability.
5. Supports a student team through to a pilot.
6. Records measurable outcomes with industry support.
